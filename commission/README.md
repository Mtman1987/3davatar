# Neon Gamer facial proof of concept

Tested complete character: four facial morphs (jawOpen, mouthSmileLeft, mouthSmileRight, browInnerUp), thirteen body clips and sixty-six skin joints. No blinking or full ARKit52 yet. This is a prototype, not a completed facial-animation commission.

Download the editable Blender file, GLB, preview images, scripts and test evidence here:
https://storage.googleapis.com/runable-templates/cli-uploads%2FhmmMGvMLLTqYqUqC8JWzw8Jcf9txQIyW%2FYJFw1pTew-dqgvGNs6oZZ%2Fneon-gamer-commission-poc.zip

Package SHA-256: 0ece0e75b29ef5a8ef0c937bd870d855d7e101526151b3695eb1265f77ecec5f

The original GLBs remain unchanged. Khronos glTF Validator reported zero errors and three warnings. All original/exported bone positions matched within 0.003 mm at five sampled times per clip after preserving the 30fps source timing. Destination-app validation and complete speech/eyelid controls are still needed.
